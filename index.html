About Me.

Biographical Summary

Welcome to my academic homepage! I am an empirical environmental policy researcher pursuing a Master of Environmental Policy (iMEP) at Duke University. My work sits at the intersection of environmental and land-use economics, climate adaptation, and spatial econometric measurement.

I use causal inference methods (Difference-in-Differences, synthetic controls, panel regression) combined with satellite remote sensing (Sentinel-2, Landsat, nighttime lights) to identify how macroeconomic shocks, trade policies, and regulatory mechanisms reshape environmental and ecological outcomes.

PhD Applications: I am actively applying to fully-funded PhD programs in environmental and resource economics, public policy, and sustainability. Open to research collaborations and academic exchanges!
