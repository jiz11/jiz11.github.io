About Me.

Biographical Summary

Welcome! I am a research assistant at Duke Kunshan University, pursuing a Master of Environmental Policy (iMEP) at Duke University. My works involves skills in GIS, remote sensing, ecology and economics.

I have interest in monitoring forest biodiversity and understanding the interaction between sustainable development and biodiversity. Specifically, I have interest on three topics:
1. Developing remote sensing-based forest biodiversity data.
2. Forest biodiversity and socioeconomic development.
3. Biodiversity-adjusted forest natural capital assessment.

PhD Applications: I am actively applying to fully-funded PhD programs in GIS, remote sensing, ecology, biodiversity, environmental economics, and sustainability.
