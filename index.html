About Me

Welcome! I am an empirical researcher working at the intersection of environmental economics, land-use policy, and spatial data science. My research investigates how macroeconomic shocks, trade policies, and regulatory mechanisms reshape ecological and resource systems.

I hold a Master's degree in Environmental Policy from Duke University. My methodological toolkit combines causal inference econometric models (Difference-in-Differences, synthetic controls, panel regression) with satellite remote sensing (Sentinel, Landsat, nighttime lights).

Prospective Students / Collaborators: I am actively applying to fully-funded PhD programs in environmental and resource economics, public policy, and sustainability. Feel free to reach out via email for collaboration or discussion!
